A passive attack attempts to collect and exploit information from the system
without affecting normal system access
. Legitimate users of the data typically do not notice such activities.
The method used in passive attacks is eavesdropping and monitoring transmitted information, with the main goal being to
obtain the transmitted data
.
Characteristics of local area network (LAN) data transmission: Broadcasting
被动攻击试图收集、利用系统的信息但
不影响系统
的正常访问，数据的合法用户对这种活动一般不会觉察到
被动攻击采取的方法是对传输中的信息进行窃听和监测，主要目标是获得传输的
信息

: Responsible for monitoring network traffic, detecting malicious activities, installing
firewalls, intrusion detection systems (IDS)

IT Administrators
: Ensuring system security and stability by regularly updating operating systems and software patches, configuring security policies (such as strong password policies and access controls).

C-I-A Triad
Confidentiality:
一种能够确保资源只被授权的当事人查看的系统能力

The integrity of an object refers to the fact that the object is:

Modified only by authorized entities
一个对象的完整性，指该对象是：

=== Slide 20 (ppt/slides/slide27.xml) ===
Integrity
以下是完整性被破坏的几个例子：
数据篡改
：黑客通过未经授权的方式修改数据库中的记录，改变了数据的原始内容。例如，修改了金融交易记录，导致账目错误。
软件篡改
：恶意软件感染计算机系统并修改了原本正常的程序代码，导致程序行为异常或泄露敏感信息。
网络中间人攻击
：攻击者拦截并修改在网络传输中的数据包，例如篡改用户登录凭证或支付信息，从而达到非法目的。
权限滥用
：某些用户或应用程序拥有不应有的权限，修改了系统配置文件或关键数据，导致系统功能受损或数据丢失。
27

Availability
Availability
:
refers to the property of a system, service, or resource being accessible and operational when needed.
服务和系统便于使用
,
并且在预期的方式下执行

=== Slide 22 (ppt/slides/slide29.xml) ===
